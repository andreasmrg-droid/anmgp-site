Trench League

A brutal top-down armored sport. Win the spiked steel ball with your fists, knock rivals out cold and fight your way across the solar system.

  • Two-button controls: easy to pick up, hard to survive
  • 8 worlds, 64 clubs, leagues, playoffs and a Solar Final
  • The Cut: an illegal midweek knockout
  • Local hot seat and online play: 1 vs 1, co-op, 2 vs 2, cups

Automotive Empire

A deep motorsport management sim. Start with one kart and climb the whole pyramid while 21 championships run at once.

  • 21 championships across 7 disciplines
  • 172 rival teams on the same calendar
  • Five R&D pillars — and never enough budget
  • From karting to GP, rally, stock cars and endurance
