Trench League

A brutal, top-down armored sport. Punch your way to the ball, break the line and smash it home — from Earth to Triton.

  • Two-button controls: easy to pick up, hard to survive
  • 8 worlds, 64 clubs, leagues, playoffs and a Final
  • Power-ups: Freeze, Strength, Speed, Steal
  • 1v1 hot seat and Hot Seat Cups
