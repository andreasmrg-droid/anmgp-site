The game

Start with one kart. Take the whole pyramid.

Automotive Empire runs the whole motorsport pyramid as one living world. Karting, rally, touring cars, stock cars, endurance, open wheel and GP: 21 championships advance together, week by week.

You start with one kart team. 172 rival teams recruit, research and spend on the same calendar. Design every part, build it in your own plant, sign the people who make the difference, and sell road cars to pay for the racing.

  • Seven disciplines, all at once: karting, rally, touring cars, stock cars, endurance prototypes, open wheel and GP.
  • 172 rival teams on the same calendar, recruiting and researching while you do.
  • Five R&D pillars: Design, Upgrade, Reverse Engineering, Special Projects, Commercial Cars. You will never fund all five.
  • Build parts in your own CNC plant and homologate them. Every upgrade trades performance against reliability.
  • Drivers and staff with contracts, loyalty and ambition: designers, team principals, strategists, pit crews and a CFO.
  • Lap-by-lap race days with weather, tyres, safety cars and real-time pit calls.
  • The road car pays for the race car: a live car market, a parts market and a campus of up to 20 buildings.
  • Mod-friendly by design.
