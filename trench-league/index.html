The game

Blood. Steel. Obedience.

In 2300 the Solar Government does not ban the Trench. It funds the lights and puts the Eye, a Census camera, in every wall. Nine armoured players a side, one spiked steel ball, and a crowd that wants blood more than goals.

Win the ball with your fists, knock rivals out cold and watch the Reclaim drag them off the pitch. Run a club across 8 worlds and 64 teams, survive the illegal midweek Cut, and settle it with friends on one screen or online.

  • Two buttons do everything. Easy to pick up, hard to survive.
  • Win the ball with your fists. Knock a rival out cold and the Reclaim drags them off the pitch.
  • Hit the Eye for points and charge the ball at the Tesla towers.
  • Four formations, each one beats another and fears another.
  • 64 clubs on 8 worlds, from Earth to Triton: leagues, playoffs and a Solar Final.
  • The Cut: an illegal midweek knockout. Win, and strike a loser’s player off their file.
  • Wounds, ageing and the Exit List, plus Ironman careers.
  • Fight your friends: 1 vs 1 and co-op on one screen, the Hot Seat Cup, and online play over Steam or PC to PC (1 vs 1, co-op, 2 vs 2, cups up to 32 players, ranked ladder).
